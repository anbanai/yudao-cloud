# CMP-99 R01 correction verification

Date: 2026-11-14

## Implemented

- V01 creates `marketing_point_lifecycle_backfill` and records each account's
  pre-expiry balance, expired remainder and post-expiry balance in the same
  transaction as the backfill.
- V01 rejects an account whose current balance is lower than its expirable
  ledger before changing member balances.
- R01 copies the snapshot into a retry journal and updates the member balance
  and journal state in one DML transaction. A committed journal row is not
  applied twice after an interruption.
- R01 verifies the expected pre/post balance on every retry, makes lifecycle
  column/index drops conditional, and removes journals only after all DDL
  succeeds.
- The disposable MySQL harness now covers the inconsistent-balance preflight
  and an injected interruption after compensation followed by an R01 retry.
